# The taste library

The art director reads this before writing any direction. It anchors the work
to specific good work instead of to "church website," which is how a studio
with no references drifts to the category average.

## What an entry holds

Each entry is a note, never an image. Images are never committed to this
repository. Reference images an art director gathers for a church are saved
in that church's private scratch path (`.handbuilt/build-my-brand/scratch/references/`)
and never leave it.

```markdown
## <Name of the work>

- **What it is:** one line, with a public link where there is one.
- **Tags:** church or not; web, print or social; with photos or without.
- **What it does well:** two or three lines.
- **What to take:** the principle, not the look.
- **What not to copy:** the parts that belong to its owner.
```

## Entries

## A church with a near-colorless site and loud series art

- **What it is:** A large church whose website is almost colorless while each
  sermon series gets its own complete look: its own type, texture and color.
- **Tags:** church; web and social; with photos.
- **What it does well:** Every thumbnail keeps the same bones (a real photo
  of the speaker, a huge title, a small logo in a corner). Inside a series,
  each week changes one thing.
- **What to take:** Fixed bones, free campaigns. The bones are the brand; the
  rest is creative.
- **What not to copy:** Its type, art or layouts.

## Brand pieces built from one color and a neutral

- **What it is:** A pattern across strong identity work in design showcases:
  one deep color and cream, one field color with black type and a photo.
- **Tags:** not church; print and social; with and without photos.
- **What it does well:** Richness comes from proportion (a big field of the
  one color, or a tiny bit of it), from big type and from a good photo, not
  from a bigger palette. The one piece that shows a grid of swatches is the
  dullest.
- **What to take:** Three brand colors are enough. Campaigns take extra
  colors from their own photos.
- **What not to copy:** Any specific piece.

## The average church website

- **What it is:** The look most small church sites share: a stock hero of a
  sunrise or hands, a centered welcome, three cards for worship, kids and
  giving, the same blue.
- **Tags:** church; web; with photos.
- **What it does well:** Nothing a visitor remembers.
- **What to take:** A list of what to avoid. If a page could belong to any
  church, it fails.
- **What not to copy:** All of it.
