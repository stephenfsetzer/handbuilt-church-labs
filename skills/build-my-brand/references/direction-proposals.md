# Direction proposals

## Building directions

Build two or three directions from the brief, the discovery audit, and the
church's tradition. Each direction is a whole idea the pastor can compare:

- **Name.** Two or three words that hold the idea.
- **Story.** One paragraph a stranger could feel from across the street. Draw
  it from what the pastor actually said, not from adjectives you supplied.
- **Palette.** Four to six colors with jobs, shown as swatches.
- **Type territory.** A display face and a text face, both open-licensed.
- **Reference board.** Five or more named pieces of real work the direction
  learns from, with one line each on what to take.
- **Real applications in type and color.** At least the bulletin cover, the
  church's own test announcement (the program image an agent would be asked
  for in an ordinary week), and a social post in the feed, set in the
  direction's type and palette with a plain placeholder where the mark will
  go. Add how an agent would make images in this direction, and where the
  kept mark limits the idea on the refresh path.

Directions must differ in idea, not only in color. If two directions would
feel the same to a stranger, replace one.

Each board is a mood board, not a design. Keep it visibly rough: label the
pieces "sketch", use loose type and flat placeholders, and leave out
polish such as finished photo treatment and pixel-perfect layout. A board
that looks finished makes the pastor judge everything at once; this round
only asks which idea is right. Finish starts at develop, and the interface
will not let a later presentation show fewer pieces than these boards.

Photographs of people stay true to life: natural color or plain black and
white. A strong palette color laid over a photo (a duotone, a heavy tint)
hides faces and makes the board hard to judge, so put the color beside or
behind the photo instead. Shapes and type never cover a face.

The creative director presents the round and opens it "Creative director ·
Your direction". The researcher builds each direction's reference board, and
the board says so, so the pastor sees who did what.

Website screens, signage plans and print layouts are not applications of
the brand round. They come after approval, as their own work, and the
interface refuses a board that shows one.

## Marks

No mark is shown in the direction round; on the refresh path the kept mark
appears unmodified. Once the pastor picks a direction, the senior designer
develops it as a whole system with the mark inside it; see
[the studio](studio/README.md). Image generation is optional there and
never required. Save every board the pastor sees under
`brand/staging/explorations/` so the record survives.

## Showing options

Put the options side by side in one place, under one line that says what is
asked: "These are directions, not designs. Tell me what pulls you and what
doesn't on each." For each, say in one line what it says about the church.
First ask for reactions, a like and a dislike per board. Then say what the
pick commits (the idea) and what stays open (color, type and finish are
worked out inside that idea), and ask "which of these is the church you are
trying to be," not "which do you like."

The pick may combine boards ("B's voice with C's people"). Record the
combination as the decision, say in one line what comes from each board,
and confirm that line with the pastor before develop. Write the rationale
from their reactions and pick, or from the brief, and record it.

## Recording

Metadata carries `options` (one line per direction), `applications` (the
keys shown on every board, at least `bulletin_cover`, `test_announcement`,
`social_post`), `boards` (the board images under `brand/`), the
`decision`, and the `rationale` in the pastor's words. `references` may
list the board's named work with `name`, `learn`, and a `url`.

## Refresh path

When the fork is `refresh`, the mark is kept. Directions are still built,
because the idea, palette, type, and voice still need deciding, but each
direction's applications show the kept mark unmodified in that direction's
type and color. Say honestly where the kept mark limits a direction, and
remember the pastor's `preserve` answer is the only protection on it.
