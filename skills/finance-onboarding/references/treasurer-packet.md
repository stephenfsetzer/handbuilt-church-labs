# Treasurer packet (stage 3)

One page for the treasurer and bookkeeper, saved as
`finance/onboarding/treasurer-packet.md` (and a PDF if they want one).

## Shape

- Open with what is working and thanks for it (for example, a recent
  reconciliation). The treasurer is a partner, not a subject of review.
- Then each open readiness item, in the checklist's order:
  - **What we noticed**, from the data, with the figure and date.
  - **What would help**, as a change in their own books (for example, record
    Stripe payments gross, with fees as an expense, within two weeks of
    month end; lock each month after reconciling).
  - **Why it matters to the board**, in one sentence.
- Close with the calendar question: when could books be closed each month,
  and when could the report be ready?

## Rules

- Suggestions and questions, not instructions. The bookkeeper decides how to
  work in their own system.
- No accusations. Restricted-fund questions are asked, not asserted.
- The agent never makes the changes, and never logs in to do them.
