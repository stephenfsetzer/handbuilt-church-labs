# Finance overview (stage 1)

Written for the pastor, from `finance/onboarding/findings.json`. Saved as
`finance/finance-overview.md`. Plain language; lead with what matters; every figure names its source
and date; estimates are labeled.

## Sections

1. **Summary.** Five or six numbered findings, most important first: can we
   pay our bills, the year's direction, the biggest dependency, anything that
   changes what "cash" means (restricted funds), how trustworthy the books
   are today.
2. **Systems and how money moves.** QuickBooks, the bank, payment services,
   payroll, investments: what each does and what connects to what. Who has
   access (ask; do not guess).
3. **Accounts and balances.** Everyday bank accounts, payment services,
   investments, liabilities, fund balances, each with a note.
4. **Where money comes from and goes.** The draft groups from the findings,
   twelve months.
5. **What can be trusted today.** A table: figure, reliable or not, why.
6. **Questions for the treasurer.** From the findings, plus the four asks
   (reconciliation, month lock, budget, access).
7. **What comes next.** The four onboarding stages, and which items will
   become the readiness checklist.

## Tone

This is the pastor's private working document, not a board paper. Say what
the books show and what they cannot show. Never conclude that money was
misused; name what needs confirming and who can confirm it.
