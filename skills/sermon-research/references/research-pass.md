# Research pass

Read these methodology sections first: Research posture, Research depth and
execution, and Contemporary Convergence, plus the source-verification ledger
schema and claim-support rules. This pass and the writing pass run in the
same context: you open the sources, keep compact working cards, and then
write the brief yourself. The cards are your notes and the seed of the
source ledger, and the editor uses them to locate evidence for independent
rechecking. Do not delegate a separate writing pass. Do not write a finished
brief until the sufficiency judgment is made.

Start with two to four live questions raised by the passage. Find arguments
that change how those questions can be answered. Examine the relevant context
of substantial scholarship and historical Christian interpretation. Honor
church preferences without using them to predetermine the conclusion.

For each materially useful source, make a compact evidence card:

- Source ID, author, work, date, direct URL or citation, and exact section,
  page, verse, or timestamp. Distinguish original and republication dates.
- Access: what was actually opened; any limit on the portion available.
- Argument: what the author claims and the textual or theological reasons.
- Qualification: what the author limits, concedes, or leaves unresolved.
- Contribution: which research question changes because of this argument.
- Researcher's inference, explicitly separated from the author's position.

Every argument or qualification sentence on a card carries a locator the
writer could reopen (section, page, paragraph, or timestamp), and a short
exact excerpt where wording matters. A sentence you cannot pin to a locator
is your inference: label it as such or delete it. Do not extend a source's
theme into a claim it does not make; the commonest research error is a
plausible statement the source never says.

Summarize in your own words and observe source-specific quotation and summary
limits. Preserve enough context to prevent a writer from attaching an attractive
claim to an unrelated source. Use a brief exact excerpt only when wording is
material and permitted. Raw scraped pages need not be passed between agents;
locators and compact arguments should let a reviewer reopen the evidence.

Add a question map showing where readings agree, differ in emphasis, or conflict.
Do not manufacture disagreement. Include the contemporary shortlist, selected
work's cultural significance and supporting evidence, and why alternatives
were rejected. Follow the ranked media and recency rules in the methodology.
When human sciences are enabled, add a card for the one finding or review
that changes how the passage's human difficulty is understood, opened at its
locator, with the methodology's limits stated; when nothing strong surfaces,
say so.

End with a sufficiency judgment. Do the cards support an actual interpretive
conversation beyond Bible text, technical notes, and short devotional summaries?
Which consequential gap still needs retrieval? A priority theologian's thin
outline can be omitted if it contributes no developed argument. No quota of
names substitutes for depth. A gap requiring evidence returns to research
before drafting. Tell the lead what is missing, rather than padding the cards.

Before writing, judge sufficiency honestly; a thin card set sends you back to
retrieval before any prose. Preserve source IDs for the final source ledger.

## Speed and size

An interactive run should reach `research_complete` in under ten minutes,
so keep retrieval parallel and the cards compact:

- Issue independent fetches and searches together in one turn, never one
  per turn. Once the leads are known, open the Bible text, the historic
  reading, the scholarly reading, and the commentary in the same batch.
- Keep each card to about 100 words plus its quotations and locators. Six
  to eight cards is plenty. A card's job is an argument with a locator, and
  the cards together seed the ledger; they need no prose between them.
- Keep the research log as a tally of at most 20 lines: queries run, pages
  opened, pages blocked, cultural and human-sciences actions used, any
  conservative choice made while the pastor was away, and every blocked
  `record` attempt with its error code and the fix. No narrative table.
