About our parish

St. Aidan's Episcopal Church is a parish of the Episcopal Church in the Diocese of the Western Riverlands. We were planted by river families who wanted a place to pray together, and we still keep the doors open on weekdays for anyone who needs a quiet place.

How we worship

We worship with the Book of Common Prayer (1979), Holy Eucharist, Rite II, and we follow the Revised Common Lectionary through the church year.

Our rector is the Rev. Miriam Okafor. See our clergy and staff.
