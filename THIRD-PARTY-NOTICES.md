## Sunday worship text

The Episcopal Sunday library is drawn from the English 1979 Book of Common
Prayer, using the official Episcopal Church PDF with the 2006 Revised Common
Lectionary update. Church Publishing identifies the Book of Common Prayer as
public domain in its [copyright guidance](https://prod.churchpublishing.org/siteassets/pdf/copyright-brochure/cpi-copyright-brochure-2015.pdf).
Source edition, page references, extraction details, and release checksums
are maintained under `skills/bulletin/resources/bcp1979/`. Congregation-only
prayer leaflets are identified as abridgments; their omission rubrics are
editorial guidance rather than additional BCP words. Church-specific wording
is not part of this standard source library.

The historical Thomas Ken doxology has its own source attribution in
`skills/bulletin/renderer/liturgy/doxology.md`.

Modern Evangelical Lutheran Worship and Lutheran Service Book text and music
are not redistributed by this plugin. [Augsburg Fortress permissions](https://www.augsburgfortress.org/info/permissions)
limit ordinary liturgy licenses to church-specific worship use.
[Concordia's Liturgy License Agreement](https://music.cph.org/liturgy-license-agreement-lutheran-service-builder)
restricts distribution outside the licensed congregation and internet
redistribution beyond the specified worship broadcasts. These congregational
licenses do not authorize a public plugin text bundle. Permitted private
church material remains subject to the applicable publisher's terms.
