# Build My Website

Build My Website builds your church's website from your approved brand. It
starts only after Build My Brand is approved, including its front-page
proof, so the website never has to argue with the brand.

Say "Build my website." The work goes in this order, and each step is
approved before the next one starts:

1. **The site map.** The list of pages, what each one is for, who it is
   for, and what a visitor must be able to do there. You approve the list.
2. **Your words, page by page.** One sheet of five to eight questions for
   each page, such as "What do you wish every first-time visitor knew
   before they walked in?" Answer by writing or speaking, at your own pace.
   The agent also gathers your words from sermons, newsletters and
   bulletins, and says where each one came from. A website built without
   enough of your words turns every round into editing.
3. **What each page says.** Every page as plain gray boxes with your real
   words, so you approve what it says and how long it is before anyone
   reacts to design. Facts like service times and the address are written
   once and shown everywhere.
4. **The page plans.** The art director plans how each page moves from one
   section to the next, so no page turns into the same section repeated.
5. **Home and one inside page,** at computer and phone size. A critic who
   did not make them checks them first. You react once, then once more.
6. **The rest of the site,** built from the parts you approved. You see the
   whole site once.
7. **What moves.** Two or three small moments, and what happens for
   visitors who turn motion off.
8. **The built site,** checked automatically at both sizes, on a private
   preview link.
9. **Keeping it up.** One person is named to approve every change. Three
   places on the site change with the season or the week: the front page,
   an announcement band (including seasonal service times), and one
   featured page. Everything else stays put. You or a volunteer say what
   should change; the agent decides how it looks and shows the approver a
   preview first.

Once a step is approved it is fixed. If something has to change later, the
agent writes down what is changing and why, and redoes the steps after it.

Your website's working files live in `website/` in your church folder.
Nothing goes live until the named approver says yes.
